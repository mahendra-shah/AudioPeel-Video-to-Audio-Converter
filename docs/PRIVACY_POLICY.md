# Privacy Policy for AudioPeel

**Last Updated:** February 20, 2026

## Introduction

AudioPeel ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our video-to-MP3 converter application.

## Information We Collect

### Local Storage Only
AudioPeel stores the following information **locally on your device only**:
- Conversion history (video file names, output audio file paths, conversion dates, file sizes)
- App settings (audio quality preference, dark mode, auto-delete preference)
- Custom output folder path (if you've changed it)

**Important:** This information never leaves your device. We do not upload, sync, or transmit this data to any servers.

### Advertising Data (via Google AdMob)
AudioPeel displays ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may automatically collect:
- Advertising ID (a unique, resettable identifier)
- Device information (model, OS version, screen size)
- IP address (approximate location)
- Ad interaction data (views, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google according to their [AdMob Privacy Policy](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543).

## Permissions We Request

### Storage Access (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)
**Purpose:** To access videos you select for conversion and save the resulting MP3 files.
**Android 8-9 only:** These permissions are only requested on older Android versions.
**Android 10+:** No permission needed due to scoped storage.

### Modify System Settings (WRITE_SETTINGS)
**Purpose:** To set converted audio as your device ringtone (optional feature).
**When requested:** Only when you tap "Set as Ringtone" on a successfully converted file.
**User control:** This is entirely optional. You're not required to grant this permission to use the app.

### Post Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)
**Purpose:** To show conversion progress notifications when you minimize the app during conversion (Android 13+ only).
**When requested:** Before starting your first conversion (Android 13+ devices only).
**What it shows:** Progress percentage, estimated time remaining, and completion status.
**User control:** Optional. If denied, conversions still work - you just won't see notifications outside the app.
**Data:** Notifications are shown locally on your device only. No notification data is sent to servers.

### Internet & Network State (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE)
**Purpose:** To display ads via Google AdMob and optimize ad delivery.
**Note:** No other network communication occurs. The app works 100% offline for conversions.

## How We Use Information

- **Conversion History:** Displayed in the app's History screen so you can easily find and manage your converted files.
- **Settings:** Remembered across app sessions for your convenience.
- **AdMob Data:** Used by Google to display relevant ads and measure ad performance.

## Data Sharing

We **do not share, sell, or trade** your information with any third parties, except:
- **Google AdMob:** Advertising data is shared with Google for ad delivery (see their privacy policy above).

## Data Retention

- **Local Data:** Stored indefinitely on your device until you delete it (via app's delete functions) or uninstall the app.
- **AdMob Data:** Retained according to Google's policies (typically 2 months for ad serving data).

## Your Rights

- **Access:** All your data is stored locally. You can view conversion history in the app's History screen.
- **Deletion:** Delete individual conversions or clear all history via the app. Uninstalling the app deletes all local data.
- **Opt-Out of Personalized Ads:** Adjust ad preferences in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization).

## Children's Privacy

AudioPeel does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app is rated for Everyone and contains family-safe content.
