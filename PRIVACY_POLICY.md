# Privacy Policy for AudioPeel

**Effective Date:** February 22, 2026  
**Last Updated:** February 22, 2026

## Introduction

AudioPeel ("we," "our," or "us") is a video-to-audio converter application for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Files You Process
- **Video files:** When you select a video for conversion, we temporarily access the file to extract audio. Files are processed locally on your device.
- **Audio files:** Converted audio files are saved to your device's storage.
- **Storage:** We do NOT upload, store, or transmit your media files to any external servers.

### 2. Device Permissions
We request the following permissions based on your Android version:

**Android 13+ (Granular Media Permissions):**
- **READ_MEDIA_VIDEO:** To read video files you select for conversion. This is our app's core functionality.
- **READ_MEDIA_AUDIO:** To save converted audio files to your device.
- **READ_MEDIA_IMAGES:** Declared in the app manifest but not intentionally used by our app. This permission may be requested by underlying media libraries.

**Android 9-12 (Legacy Storage):**
- **READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE:** To access video files you select (replaced by granular permissions in Android 13+).
- **WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE:** To save converted audio files (not required on Android 10+).

**All Android Versions:**
- **Notification Permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS):** To show conversion progress notifications.
- **Internet Access:** Required for displaying advertisements.

### 3. Advertising Data
- We use **Google AdMob** to display ads in the app.
- AdMob may collect device identifiers, usage data, and location data for ad personalization.
- You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization).
- See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details.

### 4. App Usage Data
- **Conversion history:** We store metadata about your conversions (file names, dates, settings) locally on your device for the history feature.
- **App preferences:** Your quality settings and preferences are stored locally.
- **Crash reports:** Anonymous crash reports may be collected to improve app stability.

## How We Use Information

- **File Processing:** To convert videos to audio files as requested.
- **App Functionality:** To save your preferences, show conversion history, and display notifications.
- **Advertising:** To show relevant ads that support free app development.
- **App Improvement:** To fix bugs and enhance features.

## Data Storage and Security

- **Local Storage:** All your files and conversion history are stored ONLY on your device.
- **No Cloud Upload:** We do NOT upload your videos or audio files anywhere.
- **Data Deletion:** You can delete conversion history anytime from the History screen, or uninstall the app to remove all data.

## Third-Party Services

- Provides advertising services

- Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### FFmpeg (Open Source)

## Children's Privacy

AudioPeel is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us.

## Data Retention

- **Conversion History:** Stored on your device until you manually delete it or uninstall the app.
- **Preferences:** Stored until you reset them or uninstall the app.
- **Ad Data:** Managed by Google AdMob (see their retention policies).

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access:** View your conversion history in the app.
- **Delete:** Clear history or uninstall the app to remove all data.
- **Opt-Out:** Disable personalized ads in device settings.

## Changes to Privacy Policy
